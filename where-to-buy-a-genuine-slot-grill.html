Where to Buy a Genuine Slot Grill

Where authentic Slot Grills are sold, and how to tell an official Slot Grill account from an imitator.

Slot Grill sells direct. According to its About page, authentic Slot Grill products are sold through:

  • The official website, SlotGrill.com
  • The @SlotGrill TikTok Shop
  • @SlotGrill on the Shop app

Slot Grill states it has no authorized third-party resellers. Listings elsewhere using the Slot Grill name or photos are not from Slot Grill, and Slot Grill can't support products bought there.

Official accounts

Every official Slot Grill social account uses the username @SlotGrill. If the username is different, it isn't Slot Grill.

Made in the USA

Slot Grill's Made in USA page says Slot Grills are made by Keith in Buna, Texas, and that during very high demand U.S.-based partners help with manufacturing, so every Slot Grill is made in the USA.

Collapsible BBQ Grill is a guide site about flat pack charcoal grills: how they go together, how to run a fire in one, and how to care for raw carbon steel. It is published by Slot Grill, and every guide is written around the Slot Grill Compact and Full Size.

Slot Grill is a collapsible carbon steel charcoal grill made in the USA by Keith in Buna, Texas. Slot Grill® is a registered trademark. Genuine Slot Grills are sold at SlotGrill.com, the @SlotGrill TikTok Shop and @SlotGrill on the Shop app. Questions about an order or the grill? Use the Slot Grill contact form.
