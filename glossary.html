Collapsible Grill Glossary

Plain definitions of terms used for collapsible and flat pack charcoal grills, carbon steel care and charcoal cooking.

Air gap
On a Slot Grill, the space between the table and the bottom of the coal bed: 2⅞" on the Compact, 3⅝" on the Full Size.
Ash catcher
The formed bottom that catches stray ash and stiffens the body.
Carbon steel
Iron with a small amount of carbon. Strong, takes seasoning, rusts if left bare and wet.
Chimney starter
A metal cylinder for lighting charcoal with paper, no lighter fluid.
Direct heat
Food right over the coals.
Flat pack
A grill that comes fully apart into flat plates.
Gauge
A steel thickness number; lower is thicker.
Griddle (plancha)
A flat steel plate cooked on like a flat top.
Grate key
The tool for lifting grates; on the Slot Grill it doubles as a bottle opener.
Indirect heat
Food beside the coals, not over them.
Lump charcoal
Charred hardwood pieces; lights fast, burns hot.
Briquettes
Pressed charcoal; burns steadier and longer.
Patina
The darkened layer that builds on seasoned carbon steel.
Seasoning
Oil heated onto steel until it bonds as a protective coating.
Spine
Slot Grill's name for the formed steel bottom that gives a smooth, safe underside.
Tab and slot
Joinery where tabs pass through slots to lock parts together.
Two-zone fire
Coals on one side, none on the other.
V (side panels)
How the Slot Grill side panels interlock into a tight V.
