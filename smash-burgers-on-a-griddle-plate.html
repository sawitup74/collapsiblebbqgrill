Smash Burgers on a Collapsible Grill Griddle Plate

Crispy-edged smash burgers on a carbon steel griddle plate over charcoal, sized for a collapsible grill.

Smash burgers need a screaming-hot flat surface, which is exactly what a carbon steel griddle plate over a bed of coals gives you. Thin patties, hard press, deep crust, done in minutes.

Serves 4Time 25 minutesGrill Slot Grill Compact

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef, 80/20
  • Salt and pepper
  • 4 slices American cheese
  • 4 soft buns, buttered
  • 1 small onion, very thinly sliced (optional)
  • Pickles, mustard, sauce of choice

Method

  1. Divide the beef into 8 loose balls (2 oz each). Keep them cold.
  2. Light a full chimney and pour the coals under the griddle half. Put a grate on the other half for toasting buns.
  3. Preheat the griddle 5 to 8 minutes, until a drop of water vanishes almost instantly. Wipe on a thin film of oil.
  4. Place two or three balls on the griddle, spaced well apart. Smash each flat with a sturdy spatula (a second spatula pressing on top helps). Hold 10 seconds. Season.
  5. Optional: press a pinch of onion into the top of each patty before flipping.
  6. Cook about 2 minutes until the edges are deeply browned. Scrape under each patty and flip.
  7. Add cheese and stack two patties per burger. Cook about 1 minute more, until cooked through to 160°F.
  8. Toast buns cut-side down on the grate over the cooler side. Build and serve.

Tips

  • Smash only once, right away. Pressing later squeezes out juice.
  • A cast iron press or a second spatula gives a thinner, crispier patty.
  • Scrape the plate between batches.

Safe internal temperatures

Check the thickest part with an instant-read thermometer, away from bone. These are USDA minimum safe temperatures; tougher cuts like brisket and ribs are cooked well past them for tenderness.

FoodMinimum internal temperature
Beef, pork, lamb and veal steaks, chops and roasts145°F, then rest 3 minutes
Ground beef, pork, lamb and veal (burgers, fresh sausage)160°F
Chicken and turkey, whole, pieces or ground165°F
Fish and shellfish145°F, or flesh opaque and flakes with a fork
Egg dishes160°F; cook eggs until yolks and whites are firm
Precooked sausage, hot dogs and leftoversReheat to 165°F

Cooking notes for a collapsible grill

A collapsible grill puts food close to the coals, so things brown faster than on a tall backyard cooker. Pat food dry, oil the food rather than the grate when you can, and leave it alone until it releases. Keep a clean platter for cooked food, separate from the one that carried raw meat. And cook to temperature, not time: wind, charcoal type and how full the grill is all change the clock.

Taking this cook on the road

  • Prep and marinate at home; pack in sealed containers in cook order.
  • Raw meat sealed at the bottom of a cold cooler (40°F or below).
  • Bring two sets of tongs, a thermometer, soap and water.
  • Pack a lidded metal can for ash and follow the site's rules.

Questions people ask

What temperature should smash burgers reach?

Ground beef should reach 160°F. Thin smash patties get there fast; check one with a thermometer.
