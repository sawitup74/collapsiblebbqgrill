Collapsible Grill FAQ

Answers to common questions about collapsible charcoal grills, setup, fuel, seasoning, rust and the Slot Grill.

Every question answered across this site, grouped by topic.

How Collapsible Grills Work

Is a collapsible grill as sturdy as a fixed one?

It depends on the steel. The Slot Grill uses 12-gauge carbon steel on the Compact and 10-gauge on the Full Size, and its makers state both are strong enough for cast iron. Read: What Is a Collapsible Charcoal Grill?

Does a collapsible grill need tools?

Not the Slot Grill. It assembles by hand with tab and slot, with no tools and no hinges. Read: What Is a Collapsible Charcoal Grill?

Can tab-and-slot joints come loose while cooking?

When every tab is fully seated and the grill is level, the parts hold each other in place. Don't lift or move a lit grill; let it cool first. Read: How Tab-and-Slot Assembly Works

Which portable grill type packs smallest?

A flat pack grill usually packs smallest because the parts come completely apart and stack. The Slot Grill Compact packs to about 1 inch thick. Read: Flat Pack vs Hinged vs Fixed Charcoal Grills

Is the Slot Grill stainless steel?

No. Both models are carbon steel, and there is no stainless version. Read: Carbon Steel Grills Explained

Will a carbon steel grill rust?

Bare, wet carbon steel will rust. Light surface rust is cosmetic: clean it off and re-season. Read: Carbon Steel Grills Explained

Is 10-gauge thicker than 12-gauge?

Yes. Lower gauge numbers mean thicker steel. Read: Steel Gauge and Why It Matters on a Portable Grill

Can I put a collapsible grill on a wooden table?

Not directly. Use a non-combustible surface. The air gap reduces radiant heat, but it does not make a combustible surface safe. Read: Air Gap and Airflow in a Collapsible Grill

Setup & Fire

How long does a Slot Grill take to set up?

About 30 to 60 seconds once you've done it a couple of times, according to Slot Grill. Read: Setting Up a Collapsible Grill: First Setup, Step by Step

Which way do the end pieces go?

Either end works; just keep the SLOT GRILL text facing out. Read: Setting Up a Collapsible Grill: First Setup, Step by Step

How much charcoal should I use the first time?

Start with about half a chimney on the Compact and a little more on the Full Size. You can always add lit coals. Read: Your First Cook on a Collapsible Grill

How long until charcoal is ready?

Usually 15 to 20 minutes in a chimney, when the top coals have a light coat of gray ash. Read: Lighting Charcoal in a Flat Pack Grill

Why is a two-zone fire important on a lidless grill?

It gives you somewhere to move food when it's browning too fast, and a gentler zone to finish thick cuts without burning the outside. Read: How to Build a Two-Zone Fire in a Collapsible Grill

Can I add charcoal without removing the food?

Yes. Slide food to one half, lift the other grate half, add coals, and set it back. Read: Adding Charcoal During a Long Cook

Can I burn wood in a Slot Grill?

Yes. Both models burn charcoal or cooking wood. Follow local fire restrictions. Read: Cooking With Wood in a Collapsible Grill

Can I run a grate and a griddle at once?

Yes. The two-piece top takes one griddle plate and one grate, or two plates. Read: Cooking on Griddle Plates on a Collapsible Grill

Is a Slot Grill strong enough for cast iron?

Slot Grill states both models are strong enough for cast iron. Use appropriately sized pans that sit stably on the grates. Read: Using Cast Iron on a Collapsible Grill

Can the Slot Grill be used as a fire pit?

Yes, according to Slot Grill. Use it where fires are allowed, on a stable non-combustible surface, and never leave it unattended. Read: Using a Collapsible Grill as a Fire Pit After Dinner

Can I use a charcoal grill under a tent or awning in the rain?

No. Charcoal produces carbon monoxide and the fire is a hazard. Cook in the open. Read: Cooking in Wind, Cold and Rain on a Collapsible Grill

Recipes

What temperature should smash burgers reach?

Ground beef should reach 160°F. Thin smash patties get there fast; check one with a thermometer. Read: Smash Burgers on a Collapsible Grill Griddle Plate

Can chicken thighs go above 165°F?

Yes. 165°F is the minimum safe temperature; thighs are often more tender at 175–185°F. Read: Juicy Chicken Thighs on a Collapsible Grill

Is pink pork safe at 145°F?

Yes. USDA's safe minimum for whole cuts of pork is 145°F with a 3-minute rest, and it may still look slightly pink. Read: Grilled Pork Tenderloin on a Collapsible Grill

Care & Storage

How many coats of seasoning?

Two or three thin coats to start, then maintain with a light coat of oil after cooks. Read: Seasoning a New Carbon Steel Collapsible Grill

Why is my seasoning sticky?

Too much oil. Wipe thinner and heat longer. Read: Seasoning a New Carbon Steel Collapsible Grill

Can I wash a carbon steel grill with soap?

A little mild soap is fine occasionally; dry immediately and re-oil. Read: Cleaning a Collapsible Grill After a Cook

Is surface rust dangerous on a grill?

Light surface rust is cosmetic. Clean it off and re-season before cooking. Read: Surface Rust on a Carbon Steel Grill: Prevent It and Fix It

How flat does the Slot Grill pack?

About 1 inch for the Compact and about 1.25 inches for the Full Size. Read: Packing Flat and Storing a Collapsible Grill

How long does charcoal ash stay hot?

It can stay hot for many hours. Treat it as hot until it's cold to the touch. Read: Charcoal Ash: Safe Handling and Disposal

Choosing a Collapsible Grill

Which Slot Grill is lighter?

The Compact, at about 14 lb versus about 27.6 lb for the Full Size (grill only). Read: Compact vs Full Size Slot Grill: Which Collapsible Grill Fits You?
