Flat pack charcoal, explained

The collapsible BBQ grill, from flat pack to first sear

A collapsible charcoal grill is a stack of steel plates that locks into a firebox by hand and comes apart again for the trunk or the shelf. These guides cover how that works, how to light and manage a fire in one, how to care for raw carbon steel, and what to cook. Every guide is written around the Slot Grill, a tab-and-slot carbon steel grill made in Buna, Texas.
