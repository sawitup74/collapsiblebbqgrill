Grilled Pork Tenderloin on a Collapsible Grill

A whole pork tenderloin seared over charcoal and finished on the cool side of a flat-pack grill, to a safe and juicy 145°F.

Pork tenderloin fits a compact grill perfectly: long and slim, it cooks in about 20 minutes. The trick is pulling it at 145°F and resting it so it stays moist.

Serves 3–4Time 35 minutesGrill Slot Grill Compact

Ingredients

  • 1 pork tenderloin, about 1–1¼ lb, silver skin trimmed
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp ground cumin or dried thyme
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced

Method

  1. Mix the seasonings with the oil and rub over the pork. Let sit 15 minutes while the fire lights.
  2. Build a two-zone fire.
  3. Sear on all sides over the coals, about 2 minutes per side, 8 minutes total.
  4. Move to the cool side and cook, turning every few minutes, until the thickest part reads 145°F, about 8 to 12 more minutes.
  5. Rest 3 minutes, then slice into medallions.

Tips

  • The thin tail end cooks faster; keep it toward the cooler edge.
  • Lay it diagonally if it's too long for the cooking area.

Safe internal temperatures

Check the thickest part with an instant-read thermometer, away from bone. These are USDA minimum safe temperatures; tougher cuts like brisket and ribs are cooked well past them for tenderness.

FoodMinimum internal temperature
Beef, pork, lamb and veal steaks, chops and roasts145°F, then rest 3 minutes
Ground beef, pork, lamb and veal (burgers, fresh sausage)160°F
Chicken and turkey, whole, pieces or ground165°F
Fish and shellfish145°F, or flesh opaque and flakes with a fork
Egg dishes160°F; cook eggs until yolks and whites are firm
Precooked sausage, hot dogs and leftoversReheat to 165°F

Cooking notes for a collapsible grill

A collapsible grill puts food close to the coals, so things brown faster than on a tall backyard cooker. Pat food dry, oil the food rather than the grate when you can, and leave it alone until it releases. Keep a clean platter for cooked food, separate from the one that carried raw meat. And cook to temperature, not time: wind, charcoal type and how full the grill is all change the clock.

Taking this cook on the road

  • Prep and marinate at home; pack in sealed containers in cook order.
  • Raw meat sealed at the bottom of a cold cooler (40°F or below).
  • Bring two sets of tongs, a thermometer, soap and water.
  • Pack a lidded metal can for ash and follow the site's rules.

Questions people ask

Is pink pork safe at 145°F?

Yes. USDA's safe minimum for whole cuts of pork is 145°F with a 3-minute rest, and it may still look slightly pink.
